This follows tightening moves from the Federal Reserve and the European Central Bank. For Japanese savers, this is welcome news—finally, deposits earn real returns. For borrowers, especially companies and governments, it's a headwind. And globally, it signals that central banks worldwide are willing to squeeze economic growth to combat inflation. The question now is whether his investment philosophy and management approach will survive his departure.
