Morning Briefing: Finance & Geopolitics

An automated ~8-10 minute daily audio briefing, delivered as a podcast feed.

Feed URL — add this to your podcast app:
https://jakubpechoucek-glitch.github.io/news-podcast/feed.xml

How to subscribe:

  1. Open your podcast app (Apple Podcasts, Overcast, Pocket Casts, Spotify...)
  2. Find "Add a show by URL" / "Add by RSS feed"
  3. Paste the feed URL above

A new episode is generated every morning by this pipeline: RSS news feeds → Claude (writes the script) → edge-tts (voice) → this podcast feed.
